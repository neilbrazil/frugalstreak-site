A no-spend tracker that is only a streak

Tap a Home Screen widget on every day you get through without an impulse buy, and watch the days fill in on a calendar. There is no budget to set up and no bank account to connect.

What it does

Log the day from your Home Screen

The whole widget is a button. Tap it once and today is logged, without FrugalStreak ever opening. Control Center and Siri work too: say "Log today in FrugalStreak".

You decide what counts

Most people don't count rent, bills or the weekly shop. Write your own one-line rule during setup and the app shows it back to you. Nothing is categorised and nothing is scored, so there is no argument with yourself at 9pm about whether a coffee broke the rule.

The run of days is the point

A calendar of the last five weeks sits on the first screen, next to the current count. Forgot to log yesterday? Tap it in the calendar. Watching a block of days fill in is the reason streaks work at all, so it is the first thing you see.

A reminder only when you need one

Turn on the evening reminder and it arrives only on days you haven't logged, with a Log button in the notification itself. In the evening, the app and widgets also show when today is still open.

Badges at 3, 7, 14, 30 and 100 days

Five milestones, earned from your best streak rather than your current one, so a reset can never take one back. They are free, and so is the celebration when you reach one.

Being honest costs you nothing

Tap "I spent today" and the current streak goes to zero, which is the whole point of a streak. Your best-ever run is kept and stays on screen. An app that punished you for telling the truth would just teach you to lie to it.

No account and no bank connection

There is no sign-up and no server. Your streak lives on your device and the app works with the network switched off. Nothing about your spending is transmitted anywhere, because there is nothing to transmit.

iPhone, iPad and iPhone Duo

On iPad and on the unfolded iPhone Duo, the calendar sits beside your streak, and the layout follows as you fold and unfold. FrugalStreak is available in English, German, French and Spanish.

Common questions

Is FrugalStreak free?
Yes. Logging today or yesterday as a no-spend day, your streak count, the calendar, the milestone badges and the evening reminder are free with no time limit. A one-time $2.99 unlock adds your full streak archive, the medium and large calendar widgets, custom widget colours and logging any earlier day, and removes the small banner advert shown to free users.
Do I have to connect my bank account?
No, and you cannot. FrugalStreak has no bank linking, no transaction import and no server. It never sees a penny of your actual spending. You tell it whether today was a no-spend day and that is the entire input.
What counts as spending?
Whatever you decide. During setup you write a single line describing your own rule, and the app shows it back to you rather than judging it. Most people exclude rent, bills and groceries, and count everything discretionary.
What happens when I break the streak?
Tap "I spent today" and the current streak resets to zero. Your longest-ever streak is kept and stays visible, and any badges you have earned stay earned. Resetting honestly never costs you your record.
Do I need to open the app every day?
No. Add the widget to your Home Screen and one tap logs the day with the app closed. You can also log from Control Center or by asking Siri.
Will it remind me?
Only if you want it to. The evening reminder is optional, and it only arrives on days you haven't logged yet. You can log straight from the notification.
Does it work on iPad and iPhone Duo?
Yes. On iPad and on the unfolded iPhone Duo, the calendar sits beside your streak, and the layout adjusts as you fold and unfold the phone.
Is there a subscription?
No. There is one optional purchase at $2.99 and you own it. Nothing renews.
Can I track more than one thing?
No. FrugalStreak tracks one no-spend streak, and the constraint is deliberate. If you want categories and budgets there are good apps for that, built on the opposite bet to this one.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything except the optional purchase works with no network connection, because your streak is stored on the device.
