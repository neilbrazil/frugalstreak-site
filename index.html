What it does

Log the day from your Home Screen

The whole widget is a button. Tap it once and today is logged, without FrugalStreak ever opening. That is the shortest possible distance between getting through a day and recording that you did.

You decide what counts

Most people don't count rent, bills or the weekly shop. Write your own one-line rule during setup and the app shows it back to you. Nothing is categorised and nothing is scored, so there is no argument with yourself at 9pm about whether a coffee broke the rule.

The run of days is the point

A calendar of the last five weeks sits on the first screen, next to the current count. Missed logging a day? Tap it in the calendar. Watching a block of days fill in is the reason streaks work at all, so it is the first thing you see rather than something buried in a report.

Being honest costs you nothing

Tap "I spent today" and the current streak goes to zero, which is the whole point of a streak. Your best-ever run is kept and stays on screen. An app that punished you for telling the truth would just teach you to lie to it.

Badges at 7, 30 and 100 days

Three milestones, earned from your best streak rather than your current one, so a reset can never take one back. They are free, and so is the celebration when you hit one.

No account and no bank connection

There is no sign-up and no server. Your streak lives on your device and the app works with the network switched off. Nothing about your spending is transmitted anywhere, because there is nothing to transmit.

FrugalStreak Unlocked

Everything above is free with no time limit. The paid unlock adds two things for people who stay with it long enough to want a record.

  • Your full streak history: every run you have finished, with its length and dates, not just the one you are on
  • Custom widget colours
  • Logging, the streak count, the calendar and the badges all stay free

$2.99, paid once. There is no subscription.

Common questions

Is FrugalStreak free?
Yes. Logging today or yesterday as a no-spend day, your streak count, the calendar and the milestone badges are free with no time limit. A one-time $2.99 unlock adds your full streak history, custom widget colours and logging any earlier day, and removes the small banner advert shown to free users.
Do I have to connect my bank account?
No, and you cannot. FrugalStreak has no bank linking, no transaction import and no server. It never sees a penny of your actual spending. You tell it whether today was a no-spend day and that is the entire input.
What counts as spending?
Whatever you decide. During setup you write a single line describing your own rule, and the app shows it back to you rather than judging it. Most people exclude rent, bills and groceries, and count everything discretionary.
What happens when I break the streak?
Tap "I spent today" and the current streak resets to zero. Your longest-ever streak is kept and stays visible, and any badges you have earned stay earned. Resetting honestly never costs you your record.
Do I need to open the app every day?
No. Add the widget to your Home Screen and one tap logs the day. The streak updates and the app stays closed.
Is there a subscription?
No. There is one optional purchase at $2.99 and you own it. Nothing renews.
Can I track more than one thing?
No. FrugalStreak tracks one no-spend streak, and the constraint is deliberate. If you want categories and budgets there are good apps for that, built on the opposite bet to this one.
Does it work offline?
Yes. Everything except the optional purchase works with no network connection, because your streak is stored on the device.
