The app

FrugalStreak records days on which you decide you did not spend, and shows the resulting streak. It has no bank connection and no access to your finances. What counts as spending is defined entirely by you, and the app takes no view on whether a given day qualifies.

The app is a habit tool, not financial advice. Nothing in it is a recommendation about your money, and you remain responsible for your own spending decisions.
